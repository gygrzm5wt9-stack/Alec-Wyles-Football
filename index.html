About Alec

Alec Wyles is a Class of 2028 athlete at Joe T. Robinson High School in Little Rock, Arkansas. He competes in football as a wide receiver and strong safety and wears number 67.

During his sophomore football season, Wyles recorded 1,508 receiving yards and 17 receiving touchdowns. During his sophomore basketball season, he played point guard and small forward and averaged 15.6 points, 7.2 rebounds, and 3.8 assists per game.
